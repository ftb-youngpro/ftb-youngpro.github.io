June 14, 2026 Fellowship

YP Fellowship — “Malasakit”

We sat with malasakit — compassionate care, and carrying one another's burdens the way Christ carries ours. Worship, the Word, and a whole lot of laughter with the barkada. Salamat sa lahat ng dumalo.

  • May 30, 2026YPGT

    Museum Tour — National Museum of Fine Arts

    Our latest YPGT and a day to remember. We met at 9 AM at the National Museum of Fine Arts in Ermita, wandered through Intramuros, and capped it off with café fellowship inside the old walls. Free entry, full hearts — art, history, and barkada all in one Saturday.

  • January 18, 2026Fellowship

    Goal Casting 2026

    We kicked off the year on Proverbs 16:3 — committing our 2026 plans, careers, and dreams to the Lord. The fellowship where we cast our goals and asked God to establish them.

  • February 2026Fellowship

    Fellowship Topic: “Loob”

    We looked inward — at the loob, our inner self and will — and asked what it means to let God shape who we are on the inside, not just what we achieve on the outside.

  • March 2026Fellowship

    Evangelism Workshop

    We got equipped to share the gospel naturally — at our desks, in the breakroom, wherever life puts us — learning to tell the story of Jesus in our own words and our own workplaces.

  • March 13, 2026YPGT

    KKK — Kape, Kainan, at Kwentuhan

    Coffee, good food, and unhurried conversation. Sometimes the deepest discipleship happens over a cup of kape and a long kwentuhan.

  • April 10, 2026YPGT

    Swimming Fellowship

    A summer break with the barkada — sun, water, and a whole lot of laughing. Rest is spiritual too.
