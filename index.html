Who we are

A home for young pros who love God and their work.

The Young Pro Ministry is the young professionals' department of Faith Temple Baptist Church Inc. here in Novaliches, Quezon City — a Christian community for young professionals across Metro Manila. We're the nurses on night shift, the devs debugging at 2 AM, the teachers, the freelancers, the fresh grads still figuring it out — all learning to follow Jesus right where we are.

We exist to live out our church's heartbeat: Worship God. Make Disciples. That means we don't separate "church life" from "work life." Sunday faith is meant for Monday meetings, and the same grace that saved us is the grace we carry into every deadline and every kapwa we meet.

So we gather — to worship, to open the Word, to talk through the real stuff of being a Filipino Christian in the working world, and to actually enjoy each other over kape and kwentuhan. Bring your questions, your wins, your tiredness. There's a seat here for you.

Para sa mga young pro na naghahanap ng tahanan: kung ikaw ay isang young professional na gustong lumago sa pananampalataya habang hinahabol ang career mo — nurse, teacher, dev, freelancer, o fresh grad pa lang — may upuan para sa'yo dito. Isa kaming Christian community ng mga propesyonal dito sa Novaliches, Quezon City, magkakasama sa pagsunod kay Hesus, sa kape, at sa kwentuhan. Welcome ang lahat — sumama ka.

What we do

Two ways we gather — and grow.

Every month we meet around the Word and around the table. One feeds the soul; the other builds the friendships that make faith stick.

YPF

YP Fellowship

Worship + the Word + a topic + sharing

Our regular gathering at the church — usually 2:30 PM on the 2nd or 3rd week of the month. We worship together, sit under the Word, dig into a real topic, then open up and share life with each other.

  • Worship that's heartfelt, not performed
  • A grounded message and a relevant topic for working life
  • Honest sharing and prayer in smaller circles
  • Usually 2:30 PM, 2nd or 3rd week of the month, at FTBCI
YPGT

YPGT — Young Pro Get-Together

Friendships outside the four walls

Our monthly outing — because discipleship happens over food and shared adventures too. Coffee runs, swimming, museum tours, hobby and fitness days. Come as you are, invite a friend, make memories.

  • Monthly outings: kape, food, swimming, museum tours
  • Hobby and fitness days that are invite-friendly
  • A low-pressure way to bring a friend into the family
  • Where strangers become barkada and barkada become a faith group

Discipleship themes

Our faith, in our own language.

These are the real topics we wrestle with at fellowship — Filipino values everyone knows, redeemed and re-rooted in Christ. Not flattened into English, but met where they live.

  • Loobthe inner self, the will
  • Kapwashared humanity
  • Malasakitcompassionate care
  • Bahala Nafaith-filled trust
  • Utang na Looba debt of the heart

  • Loob

    the inner self, the will

    Before God transforms our resume, He transforms our loob — renewing the hidden person of the heart until who we are inside finally matches who He's calling us to be.

  • Kapwa

    shared humanity, one-with-the-other

    To see kapwa is to recognize myself in you — and the gospel deepens it, because at the cross we are all one in our need and one in His grace.

  • Malasakit

    compassionate care

    Malasakit is love that refuses to look away; it's the same heart that moved Jesus toward the crowds, now beating in us toward every burdened co-worker and stranger.

  • Bahala Na

    faith-filled trust in God

    Not careless surrender to fate, but "Bathala na" — entrusting outcomes to the God who holds them, doing our part fully and resting in His sovereign, faithful hands.

  • Utang na Loob

    deep gratitude, a debt of the heart

    We carry the ultimate utang na loob — a debt we could never repay — answered not by guilt but by glad, lifelong gratitude to the Christ who paid it all.
