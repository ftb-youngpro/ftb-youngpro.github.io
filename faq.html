Introduction

We're the Young Pro Ministry — the young professionals' department of Faith Temple Baptist Church Inc. here in Novaliches, Quezon City. If you're a Filipino young pro looking for a Christian community where faith, friendship, and your career grow side by side, you're in the right place. Below are the questions we get asked the most.

Para sa mga young pro na naghahanap ng tahanan: kung gusto mong lumago sa pananampalataya habang hinahabol ang career mo — nurse, teacher, dev, freelancer, o fresh grad pa lang — may upuan para sa'yo dito. Hanapin mo lang ang sagot sa tanong mo, o mag-email sa amin kung wala dito.

01 · The basics

About the Young Pro Ministry

What is the FTB Young Pro Ministry?

FTB Young Pro (YP) is the Young Professionals Ministry of Faith Temple Baptist Church Inc. — a warm, faith-centered community of Filipino young professionals who worship God and make disciples together. We meet for YP Fellowship and monthly Young Pro Get-Together (YPGT) hangouts, so faith and real friendships grow side by side.

Tagalog Ang FTB Young Pro ay ang Young Professionals Ministry ng Faith Temple Baptist Church Inc. — isang warm at faith-centered na community ng mga Pinoy young pro. May YP Fellowship at monthly YPGT, para magkasabay lumago ang faith at totoong pagkakaibigan.

More about us · Join us

Is this a community or group for young professionals?

Yes! We're a Christian community and fellowship for young professionals — a group of working Filipinos chasing Jesus and their callings at the same time. Whether you're fresh out of school, navigating your first job, or already deep into your field, there's a seat for you here.

Tagalog Oo! Isa kaming komunidad at fellowship para sa mga young professionals — para sa mga nagtatrabahong Pinoy na gustong lumago sa faith kasabay ng career. Fresh grad ka man o malalim na sa field mo, may upuan ka rito.

What we do

Is there a Christian community for young professionals in Quezon City or Novaliches?

Yes — we're a Christian fellowship for young professionals based in Novaliches, Quezon City. We meet at the FTB Auditorium, #206 P. Orbe St., Blueville Subdivision, Novaliches, Quezon City. Everyone's welcome, so come as you are.

Tagalog Oo — isa kaming Christian fellowship para sa mga young pro dito sa Novaliches, Quezon City. Nagtitipon kami sa FTB Auditorium sa Blueville Subdivision. Welcome ang lahat.

Where can young professionals find faith friends and a Christian barkada?

Right here. A big part of what we do is help strangers become barkada and barkada become a faith group — through fellowships, coffee, and our monthly hangouts. If you've been looking for real, God-honoring friendships as a young professional, you're welcome to join us.

Tagalog Dito mismo. Malaking bahagi ng ginagawa namin ay tulungan ang mga estranghero na maging barkada, at ang barkada na maging faith group. Kung naghahanap ka ng totoong pagkakaibigan, welcome ka sa amin.

02 · Joining

Joining & who can come

Who can join the Young Pro Ministry?

Everyone is welcome — there's a seat saved for you. We're the young professionals (plus fresh grads and folks still figuring life out) of Faith Temple Baptist Church. Whether you're rock-solid in your faith or just curious, fresh out of school or deep into your career, you belong here. Just show up to a fellowship or YPGT, or email us first at ftb.youngpro.ministry@gmail.com.

Tagalog Welcome ang lahat — may nakareserbang upuan para sa'yo. Matibay man ang faith mo o curious ka lang, kaka-graduate ka man o matagal nang nagtatrabaho, belong ka dito. Sumipot ka lang, o mag-email muna sa amin.

How to join

Do I need to be a church member or a Christian to join?

No. You don't need to be a member of Faith Temple Baptist Church, and you don't need to be a Christian yet either. We welcome visitors, friends, and people from other churches — and plenty of people come while they're still exploring faith. Come as you are, and bring your questions.

Tagalog Hindi. Hindi mo kailangang maging member ng FTB, at hindi mo rin kailangang maging Christian. Welcome ang mga bisita at galing sa ibang simbahan. Come as you are, at dalhin mo na rin ang mga tanong mo.

There's a seat saved for you

I'm a fresh grad (or still job-hunting) — can I still join?

Absolutely. Fresh grads, students still figuring it out, people between jobs, and those just starting their careers are all welcome. You don't need a job title or a settled life to belong here — you just need to show up as you are. Come to a fellowship or email us at ftb.youngpro.ministry@gmail.com.

Tagalog Syempre. Welcome ang mga fresh grad, estudyante, mga nasa pagitan ng trabaho, o nagsisimula pa lang. Hindi mo kailangan ng job title o ayos na buhay para mabilang dito.

Join us

Pwede ba akong sumali kahit hindi pa ako Christian?

Yes — you don't need to be a Christian to come. You're genuinely welcome whether you're rock-solid in your faith or just curious and exploring. We're not here to pressure you; we're here for real conversations, the Word, kape, and friendship. Come as you are.

Tagalog Oo — hindi mo kailangang maging Christian para makasali. Totoong welcome ka, matibay man ang faith mo o curious ka lang. Walang pino-pressure; kwentuhan, Salita ng Diyos, kape, at pagkakaibigan lang.

Come as you are

How do I join or get connected to the young pro fellowship?

Getting connected is simple: message us at ftb.youngpro.ministry@gmail.com, then drop by a YP Fellowship (usually 2:30 PM, 2nd or 3rd week of the month) or come hang out at the next YPGT outing. From there, conversations turn into friendships, and friendships into a faith group — there's a seat saved for you.

Tagalog Madali lang: mag-message ka muna, tapos dumalaw sa isang YP Fellowship (kadalasan 2:30 PM, 2nd o 3rd week ng buwan) o sumama sa susunod na YPGT. May upuang nakalaan para sa'yo.

Is it free to attend, and is it a safe space?

Yes, it's free — there's no fee to attend a YP Fellowship, and it's a warm, no-judgment space. Some YPGT outings may have a small personal cost (like your own food or a venue entrance), and we'll always say so ahead of time. Come exactly as you are, wherever you are with God or your career.

Tagalog Oo, libre — walang bayad ang pagpunta sa YP Fellowship, at walang panghuhusga dito. May ilang YPGT na baka may konting personal na gastos (pagkain o entrance), pero sasabihin namin nang maaga. Halika kung ano ka man.

Do I need to RSVP or register first?

No sign-up is required — you can just show up to a YP Fellowship or YPGT. That said, it's nice to message us first at ftb.youngpro.ministry@gmail.com so we know to look out for you and can tell you exactly where and when. For featured events you'll also see a quick RSVP button on the Events page.

Tagalog Hindi kailangan mag-sign up — pwede kang basta dumating. Pero mas maganda kung mag-message ka muna para alam naming aabangan ka. May RSVP button din sa Events page para sa featured events.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes, please do — bring a friend, bring your whole barkada! Everyone's welcome here, and some of our gatherings (like Friend Day and the YPGT outings) are built exactly for inviting friends along. The more, the merrier.

Tagalog Oo, sige lang — magdala ka ng kaibigan, isama mo na ang buong barkada! May mga gathering pa nga kami (gaya ng Friend Day) na talagang para sa pag-imbita ng mga kaibigan. Mas marami, mas masaya.

When is the YP Fellowship?

Our regular YP Fellowship is usually at 2:30 PM, on the 2nd or 3rd week of the month, at the FTB Auditorium. The exact date can shift month to month, so check our Events page for the dated poster or message us to be sure.

Tagalog Ang regular naming YP Fellowship ay kadalasang 2:30 PM, tuwing 2nd o 3rd week ng buwan. Minsan nagbabago ang exact na petsa, kaya tingnan ang Events page o mag-message para sigurado.

See the schedule

How often do you meet, and what's the difference between YP Fellowship and YPGT?

We have two regular things each month. YP Fellowship is our gathering at the church — worship, the Word, a topic, and sharing (usually 2:30 PM, 2nd or 3rd week of the month). YPGT (Young Pro Get-Together) is our monthly outing outside the church — coffee, swimming, museum tours, food, hobby and fitness — for friendship and fun. So roughly twice a month, one at church and one out and about.

Tagalog May dalawang regular kami kada buwan: ang YP Fellowship (worship + Salita ng Diyos sa church) at ang YPGT (buwanang lakad sa labas — kape, swimming, museum). Mga dalawang beses kada buwan.

Come as you are — there's no dress code. Wear whatever you're comfortable in, whether that's office clothes straight from work or your weekend casual. We care that you came, not what you wore.

Tagalog Come as you are — walang dress code. Kahit ano ka komportable, pwede na — galing-trabaho man o casual na pang-weekend. Ang importante, nandito ka.

What are the five Filipino discipleship themes?

We disciple around five Filipino heart-words and re-root each one in Christ: Loob (the inner self), Kapwa (shared humanity), Malasakit (compassionate care), Bahala Na (faith-filled trust in God), and Utang na Loob (deep gratitude). Each one has a short, warm devotional you can read on our Devotionals page.

Tagalog Limang Filipino heart-words ang ginagamit namin, bawat isa iniuugat kay Cristo: Loob, Kapwa, Malasakit, Bahala Na, at Utang na Loob. May maikling devotional para sa bawat isa.
